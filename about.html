About North Star Bakery

Good food brings people together. At North Star Bakery, we're proud to be a neighborhood gathering place where everything is made with care.

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: bring fresh, handmade baked goods to our community. What started as a passion for baking has grown into a neighborhood favorite, serving delicious breads, pastries, and cakes to families, commuters, and friends.

Whether you're stopping in for your morning pastry or picking up treats for a special occasion, we want every visit to feel like coming home.

Our Ingredients & Philosophy

We believe great baking starts with great ingredients. That's why we prioritize fresh, seasonal ingredients and work with local suppliers whenever possible.

Our bakers use time-honored techniques, preparing doughs with patience and baking in small batches to deliver the quality and flavor our customers love.
