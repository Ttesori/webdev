Welcome to North Star Bakery

Freshly baked. Made for our neighborhood.

At North Star Bakery, we believe the best days start with something fresh from the oven. From handcrafted breads to sweet treats, we bake with care and bring a little warmth to every visit.

Stop by for your morning coffee and pastry, pick up a loaf for dinner, or let us help make your next celebration special.

What We're Known For

  • Artisan Breads: Freshly baked sourdough, rustic loaves, and everyday favorites.
  • Pastries: Buttery croissants, muffins, and seasonal sweet treats.
  • Custom Cakes: Delicious cakes made for birthdays, gatherings, and celebrations.
